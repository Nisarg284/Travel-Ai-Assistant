# Kerala


## Overview

Kerala, often called "God's Own Country," is a beautiful state located on the southwestern coast of India. Known for its serene backwaters, lush greenery, hill stations, beaches, and rich cultural heritage, Kerala is one of the most popular tourist destinations in India. The state is famous for Ayurveda, traditional arts, and delicious cuisine.


## Best Time To Visit

The best time to visit Kerala is from October to March when the weather is pleasant and ideal for sightseeing and outdoor activities. Monsoon season (June to September) is preferred by visitors seeking Ayurvedic treatments and lush landscapes.


## How To Reach

- **By Air:** Major airports include Cochin International Airport, Trivandrum International Airport, and Calicut International Airport, with connections to major Indian and international cities.

- **By Train:** Kerala has an extensive railway network connecting cities like Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, Kozhikode, and Alappuzha with the rest of India.

- **By Road:** National highways and state roads provide excellent connectivity. Interstate buses and private taxis are readily available.

## Accommodation

Kerala offers accommodation options for all types of travelers:

- **Budget:** Hostels and guest houses starting from ₹800–₹2,000 per night.

- **Mid-range:** Hotels, homestays, and resorts ranging from ₹2,500–₹6,000 per night.

- **Luxury:** Premium resorts, houseboats, and beachfront properties starting from ₹8,000 per night.

## Estimated Budget

- **Mid-range Traveler:** ₹4,000–₹8,000 per day

- **Luxury Traveler:** ₹10,000+ per day


## Travel Tips

- Carry light cotton clothing as Kerala has a tropical climate.

- Keep an umbrella or raincoat handy, especially during the monsoon season.

- Book houseboats and popular resorts in advance during peak season.

- Respect local customs while visiting temples and religious sites.

- Stay hydrated and use sunscreen while exploring beaches and hill stations.

- Try authentic Kerala cuisine and Ayurvedic therapies.

- Use eco-friendly practices to help preserve Kerala's natural beauty.

[//]: # (##  Travel Tips)

[//]: # ()
[//]: # (1. Hire a car with driver — Kerala's key attractions are spread across a large area. A hired car &#40;Rs.2,500–Rs.4,000/day&#41; is the most efficient and comfortable way to explore.)

[//]: # ()
[//]: # (2. Book houseboats in advance — Especially for December–January. Compare packages carefully — a good houseboat includes all meals, AC, and a personal crew.)

[//]: # ()
[//]: # (3. Try Ayurveda authentically — Look for government-certified Ayurvedic centres &#40;Green Leaf or Olive Leaf certified by Kerala Tourism&#41;. Avoid tourist trap parlours near beaches.)

[//]: # ()
[//]: # (4. Respect temple etiquette — Non-Hindus are restricted from entering some temples &#40;like Padmanabhaswamy&#41;. Dress conservatively and follow instructions at all religious sites.)

[//]: # ()
[//]: # (5. Kerala is vast — Don't try to cover everything in one trip. Choose a theme: backwaters + beaches, or hill stations + wildlife, or culture + cuisine. A focused itinerary beats a rushed one.)

[//]: # ()
[//]: # (6. The monsoon is actually beautiful — If you don't mind rain, June–August brings extraordinary lush greenery, fewer tourists, deeply discounted rates, and the best time for Ayurvedic treatments.)

[//]: # ()
[//]: # (7. Use KSRTC buses — Kerala's state buses are reliable, clean, and very affordable for intercity travel. AC Volvo options available on major routes.)

[//]: # ()
[//]: # (8. Wildlife safaris require booking — Periyar &#40;Thekkady&#41; boat safaris and jeep safaris sell out fast. Book through the Kerala Forest Department website in advance.)

[//]: # ()
[//]: # (9. Carry light, breathable clothing — Kerala is humid year-round. Light cotton or linen is your best friend. Pack a light rain jacket for the hills.)

[//]: # ()
[//]: # (10. Learn a few Malayalam words — Locals greatly appreciate any effort: Nandri &#40;thank you&#41;, Sughamano? &#40;How are you?&#41;, Enthayirunnu? &#40;What happened?&#41; — it opens doors and smiles.)

[//]: # ()
[//]: # (11. Don't miss a Kerala Sadya — If possible, experience a full banana leaf feast at a local restaurant during Onam &#40;August–September&#41; or at a good South Indian restaurant year-round.)

[//]: # ()
[//]: # (12. Sunset at Fort Kochi — Watching the Chinese fishing nets being operated at sunset from Fort Kochi's promenade is one of Kerala's most iconic and atmospheric experiences.)

[//]: # ()
[//]: # (Kerala moves at a pace all its own — slow, gentle, and deeply restorative. Let the backwaters, the spice air, and the warmth of its people wash over you.)
